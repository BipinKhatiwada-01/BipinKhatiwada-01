**Software Engineering · Systems · Full-Stack Development**

I'm interested in the engineering behind software — how systems are designed, how their components interact, and how ideas become reliable, useful products.

## Engineering Philosophy

A few ideas guide how I approach learning and building:

- **Understand the fundamentals.** Tools and frameworks are useful, but understanding the problems they solve is more valuable.
- **Build, experiment, investigate.** Practical experience is where theoretical understanding gets tested.
- **Stay curious, remain deliberate.** Explore broadly, but develop depth through sustained work.
